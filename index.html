# Road Ledger Privacy Policy **Effective date: September 21, 2026** Road Ledger is a driver-focused application for organizing freight loads, stops, mileage, equipment, documents, detention time, estimated pay, and related work records. This Privacy Policy explains what information Road Ledger may access, how that information is used, when information may leave your device, and the choices available to you. ## 1. Information Road Ledger May Access Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Road Ledger may access or process the following information. ### Location and mileage information Road Ledger may use precise or approximate device location to: * Track loaded, deadhead, personal, shop, and other mileage * Detect arrivals at and departures from stops * Support check-in and check-out records * Create trip timelines and route histories * Estimate distance and arrival times * Maintain mileage tracking while the app is running in the foreground or background If background location is enabled, location may be processed while the app is not visible or while the device screen is off. Background location is used only for driver-requested mileage and trip-recording functions. You can disable location permission through your device settings. Some mileage, geofence, arrival, and timeline features may not work correctly without it. ### Load and business records Road Ledger allows you to enter, scan, import, or store information such as: * Load, trip, reference, work-order, purchase-order, and bill-of-lading numbers * Shipper, receiver, broker, customer, and facility information * Pickup and delivery addresses * Appointment dates and times * Commodity, weight, pieces, pallets, equipment, and rate information * Driver instructions, notes, detention records, incident records, and stop history * Truck and trailer numbers, profiles, notes, and inspection reminders * Pay rates, mileage rates, accessorial pay, and estimated earnings This information is used to provide the recordkeeping, calculation, reporting, and planning features requested by the user. ### Photos, screenshots, and documents With your permission, Road Ledger may access the camera, photo library, or files you select to: * Photograph freight paperwork * Scan dispatch documents * Scan truck or trailer numbers * Attach proof, receipts, bills of lading, or other documents * Perform document cropping and image correction * Extract text using optical character recognition * Prepare load information for review or import Road Ledger should access only the images or files you select or create through the app. ### Contacts and telephone numbers If you choose to select a telephone number from your contacts, Road Ledger may access the contact information needed to complete that action. Road Ledger does not use contacts for advertising or unrelated profiling. The app should retain only the information the user deliberately selects and saves. ### User-entered settings Road Ledger may store preferences such as: * Home terminal and time zone * Mileage and pay settings * Detention rules and rates * Notification preferences * Equipment defaults * Scanner preferences * Theme and accessibility settings * Frequently used telephone numbers or message templates ### Technical information Road Ledger or platform services used to distribute and operate the app may process limited technical information, such as: * App version * Android version * Device model * Permission status * Error, crash, and performance information * Basic installation and update information This information may be used to maintain compatibility, diagnose failures, improve reliability, and protect the app from abuse. ## 2. How Information Is Used Road Ledger may use information to: * Create and manage loads and stops * Track mileage and trip events * Record arrival, check-in, check-out, detention, and departure times * Calculate distance, estimated pay, detention, and other driver records * Generate reports and exports * Maintain equipment profiles * Display past facility information and estimated loading or unloading time * Scan and organize documents * Restore user-selected data from backups * Diagnose errors and improve app reliability * Provide notifications and reminders requested by the user Road Ledger does not sell personal information. Road Ledger does not use freight records, location history, contacts, documents, or equipment information for behavioral advertising. ## 3. On-Device Processing Road Ledger is designed to keep its primary records on the user’s device whenever practical. Features such as document recognition, local learning, calculations, equipment history, stop history, and database storage may be performed locally on the device. Deleting the app or clearing its application storage may permanently remove locally stored information unless the user previously created a backup or export. ## 4. Artificial Intelligence and External Sharing Road Ledger may allow users to share screenshots, documents, or prepared instructions with an external artificial-intelligence application selected by the user. Road Ledger does not automatically control the privacy practices of an external AI service. When you deliberately share information with another application or service: * The receiving service’s privacy policy and terms apply * The information may leave your device * The receiving service may retain or process the information * You should review documents for sensitive information before sharing them Road Ledger may allow AI-generated JSON or other structured information to be pasted or imported into the app. Users should review imported information for accuracy before saving or relying on it. Road Ledger does not guarantee the accuracy of OCR, AI-generated information, mileage calculations, pay estimates, ETA estimates, or document extraction. ## 5. Directions, Maps, Calls, Messages, and Other Applications Road Ledger may open third-party applications for functions such as: * Navigation and directions * Telephone calls * Text messages * Email * File sharing * Cloud storage * Artificial-intelligence processing Information sent to another application is governed by that application’s privacy policy. Road Ledger does not control how an external application processes, stores, or shares information after the user sends it. ## 6. Information Sharing Road Ledger may share information only in the following circumstances: ### At the user’s direction Information may be shared when the user chooses to: * Export a report * Share a document or screenshot * Open an address in a navigation application * Send a message or email * Place a telephone call * Send information to an AI application * Save a backup to a selected location * Share records with a carrier, broker, customer, dispatcher, accountant, attorney, or other person ### With service providers Limited information may be processed by service providers required to distribute, secure, diagnose, or operate the app. These providers may include Google Play, Android system services, mapping providers, crash-reporting providers, or other services included in the production version of the app. Service providers may process information under their own privacy policies and contractual obligations. ### For legal and safety reasons Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to: * Comply with applicable law or a valid legal request * Protect the rights or safety of users or others * Investigate fraud, misuse, or security incidents * Enforce applicable agreements Road Ledger does not sell or rent user records to data brokers or advertisers. ## 7. Data Storage and Retention Locally stored information remains on the device until the user: * Deletes an individual record * Deletes a load, document, profile, or attachment * Clears the app’s storage * Uninstalls the app * Restores or replaces the local database Backups and exports remain wherever the user saved or shared them. Deleting information from Road Ledger does not automatically delete copies previously exported to another device, person, application, email account, or cloud-storage provider. Technical diagnostics processed by a service provider may be retained according to that provider’s policies. ## 8. Deleting Your Information Users can delete supported loads, equipment profiles, documents, notes, and other records from within Road Ledger. Users may remove all locally stored Road Ledger information by clearing the app’s storage through Android settings or uninstalling the app. Before deleting the app or clearing storage, users should export or back up any records they want to keep. If a future version of Road Ledger introduces user accounts or server-based storage, this policy will be updated with instructions for requesting account and associated-data deletion. ## 9. Permissions Road Ledger may request Android permissions only when needed for a feature, including: * Precise or approximate location * Background location * Camera access * Photos, media, or selected-file access * Contacts access or contact selection * Notifications * Telephone or messaging functions * Foreground-service operation Permissions can be reviewed or revoked through Android settings. Revoking a permission may disable the related feature. ## 10. Data Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by Road Ledger. However, no device, application, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting access to their device, backups, exported reports, and any third-party services they use. Users should avoid sharing freight documents or personal information with recipients they do not trust. ## 11. Driver Responsibility and Sensitive Records Road Ledger is an organizational and recordkeeping tool. It is not an electronic logging device unless specifically certified and represented as one, and it does not replace legally required records, inspections, bills of lading, payroll statements, tax records, or carrier systems. Users are responsible for: * Reviewing records for accuracy * Protecting confidential freight information * Following employer and carrier policies * Complying with applicable transportation, employment, privacy, and record-retention requirements * Obtaining permission before storing information belonging to another person or company ## 12. Children’s Privacy Road Ledger is intended for adult commercial drivers, owner-operators, carriers, and other adults who manage freight or mileage records. The app is not directed to children under 13, and Road Ledger does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Road Ledger, contact the developer so the matter can be investigated. ## 13. United States Use Road Ledger is currently intended primarily for users in the United States. Users outside the United States are responsible for determining whether their use of the app complies with local privacy, employment, transportation, and data-protection laws. ## 14. Continued use of Road Ledger after an updated policy becomes effective indicates acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law. ## 15.
