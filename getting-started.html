DRIVER QUICK START

Make every mile count.

Set up Road Ledger for your next run. These steps describe the intended workflow; specific controls may differ by installed version.

01

Join the Android test

Android testing
02

Protect your data first

Before updating an existing install, create and verify an app-supported data backup. Never clear storage or uninstall just to force an update.

Backup guidance
03

Set up your truck

Review driver preferences and truck/trailer details, plus location and notification permissions where needed.

04

Create or import a load

Enter dispatch details or review scanned and AI-extracted information before saving. Check addresses, dates, appointments, and stop order.

05

Record each stop

Keep arrivals, check-ins, departures, delays, documents and miles accurate. Detention terms can vary by broker or facility.

06

Finish and review

Complete your run and verify it remains accessible in Records. Check estimated pay, exported documents and historical mileage.

DETENTION BASICS

Clock reference vs. billable time

Arrival, check-in, and appointment times are different records. For a 1:30 PM appointment and 120 minutes of free time, the calculated threshold is 3:30 PM. Actual billable detention depends on whether the driver is on site and the applicable terms. Automatic appointment-time triggers are under development; verify your installed version before relying on them.

NEED HELP?

Bring the details, not just the headache.

When reporting an issue, include the app version, Android version, what you expected, and what happened. Remove sensitive load or customer details before sending screenshots.
