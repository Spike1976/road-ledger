ROAD LEDGER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Road Ledger is a driver-focused application for organizing freight loads, stops, mileage, equipment, documents, detention time, estimated pay and related work records. This policy explains what information the app may access, how it is used and the choices available to users.

1. Information Road Ledger may access

Location and mileage information

With permission, Road Ledger may use precise or approximate location to track mileage, detect arrivals and departures, support check-in and check-out records, create trip timelines, estimate distance and arrival times, and maintain driver-requested tracking while the app is in the foreground or background. Location permission can be disabled in Android settings, but related features may no longer work correctly.

Load and business records

Users may enter, scan, import or store load numbers, reference numbers, bills of lading, purchase orders, shipper and receiver information, addresses, appointments, commodities, weights, equipment, pay information, notes, detention records and related freight information.

Photos, screenshots and documents

With permission, the app may access the camera or user-selected photos and files to scan paperwork, attach records, crop documents, extract text and prepare load information for review or import.

Contacts and phone numbers

If a user chooses to select a phone number from contacts, Road Ledger may access the information needed to complete that action. Contact information is not used for advertising or unrelated profiling.

Technical information

Road Ledger or platform services may process limited technical information such as app version, Android version, device model, permission status, installation information, crashes and performance data to maintain compatibility and reliability.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to create and manage loads and stops; track mileage and trip events; record detention and arrival times; calculate distance and estimated pay; generate exports; maintain equipment profiles; organize documents; display stop history; provide reminders; and diagnose errors.

Road Ledger does not sell personal information and does not use freight records, location history, contacts or documents for behavioral advertising.

3. On-device storage and processing

Road Ledger is designed to keep its primary records on the user's device whenever practical. Document recognition, calculations, equipment history and stop history may be processed locally. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may permanently remove local information unless the user created a backup or export.

4. AI and user-directed sharing

Road Ledger may allow a user to share documents, screenshots or prepared instructions with an external AI application selected by the user. The receiving service's privacy policy applies after information is shared. Users should review sensitive material before sending it and verify imported information for accuracy.

5. Other applications and services

Road Ledger may open third-party applications for navigation, calls, text messages, email, file sharing, cloud storage or AI processing. Information sent to another application is governed by that application's privacy policy.

6. Information sharing

Information may be shared at the user's direction, with service providers needed to distribute or operate the app, or when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect safety, investigate fraud or enforce applicable agreements. Road Ledger does not rent or sell user records to advertisers or data brokers.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains until the user deletes records, clears application storage or uninstalls the app. Backups and exports remain wherever the user saved or shared them. Users can remove all locally stored information through Android's app-storage controls. Road Ledger currently does not require an app account.

8. Permissions

Depending on the features used, Road Ledger may request location, background location, camera, selected photo or file access, contact selection, notifications, phone or messaging functions, and foreground-service operation. Permissions can be reviewed or revoked in Android settings.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Road Ledger, but no device, application, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting their devices, backups, exports and third-party accounts.

10. Driver responsibility

Road Ledger is an organizational and recordkeeping tool. It is not an electronic logging device unless specifically certified and represented as one. It does not replace legally required records, inspections, payroll statements, tax records, carrier systems or professional advice. Users are responsible for reviewing records for accuracy and following applicable laws and carrier policies.

11. Children's privacy

Road Ledger is intended for adult commercial drivers, owner-operators, carriers and freight professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, permissions, service providers or legal obligations change. The revised policy will display a new effective date.
