HELP DESK

Questions from the road.

The short answers to the stuff that matters when you're trying to finish a load, not become a full-time app troubleshooter.

Is Road Ledger available on Google Play?

Road Ledger has an Android internal-testing page. Access may be restricted to approved test accounts. Availability varies by test rollout.

How do I keep my loads when updating?

Use a supported in-app backup/export and verify it before changing versions. Install only an update signed with the same app signing key and a compatible version code. Never uninstall or clear storage as an update shortcut.

Why is a load missing from Records?

Check available filters and archived or completed records. If counts do not match, stop making risky changes and contact support with the app version and a safe diagnostic export. Do not recreate loads from guesses.

When does detention start?

The app may record appointment, arrival, and check-in times independently. Free-time and billable thresholds depend on configuration and applicable carrier or broker terms. The automatically scheduled appointment trigger is a requested feature, not verified as shipped on every installed version.

Does Road Ledger automatically bill a broker?

No. Road Ledger helps organize documentation and estimated amounts. The driver or carrier is responsible for reviewing and submitting claims under the applicable agreement.

Does the app scan dispatch paperwork?

Document scan and AI import workflows can extract or prepare details, but recognition may be imperfect. Confirm reference numbers, facility addresses, dates, weights, and stop order before saving.

Is this an electronic logging device (ELD)?

No. Road Ledger is a freight recordkeeping tool and should not be treated as a certified ELD or a substitute for required hours-of-service records.

Can I send feedback or request a feature?

Yes. Email Road Ledger support with your app version and a description of the request. Please remove personal or customer-sensitive data from screenshots.

Still stuck?

Tell us your Android version, Road Ledger version, the screen involved, and the steps that reproduce the issue.
