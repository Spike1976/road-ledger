Before you update

  1. Write down your installed version and active-load status.
  2. Export and verify a backup of your records, attachments, and app settings.
  3. Confirm the new version has the same package identity, compatible signing, and a higher version code.
  4. Install as an in-place update only when the backup and migration are verified.
  5. Check the total record count, historical loads, current load, detention and attachments after installation.

Never uninstall the old app or clear its storage to force an update.
