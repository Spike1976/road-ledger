Road Ledger Support

Need testing access, found a bug or have an idea that would make the app work better on the road? Send the details and include the app version shown in Settings.

Email support Open testing page

Joining the test

Internal testing may require the Google account email used on your Android device. Send that email to the group administrator or to spikestokes@gmail.com so it can be added to the tester list.

Reporting a problem

Include what you were doing, what you expected, what happened, the app version, phone model and Android version. Add a screenshot or screen recording when possible, but remove confidential freight information first.

Protecting your records

Create a backup before clearing app storage, uninstalling the app or installing a test build that may change the database. Keep exported files in a location you control.

AI and OCR results

Document scanning and AI imports can make mistakes. Review facility names, addresses, dates, references, weights and stop order before saving or relying on imported information.
